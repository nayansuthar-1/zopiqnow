1. INTRODUCTION

This Payment & Settlement Policy ("Policy") explains how payments, refunds, settlements, and payouts work on the Zopiq platform, operated by Hybrid Monks LLP ("Zopiq", "Company", "we", "our", or "us"). It applies to Customers, Restaurant Partners, and Delivery Partners, and supplements the Terms & Conditions, Refund & Cancellation Policy, and Partner Agreements.

2. DEFINITIONS

Advance Payment means the mandatory 40% of the total order value paid online before confirmation of a COD order.

COD means Cash on Delivery, where the Customer pays the Advance Payment online and the balance on delivery.

Commission means the platform fee payable to Zopiq in respect of items sold, being ₹10 per item unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Settlement means the amount payable to a Restaurant Partner after applicable deductions.

Payout means the amount payable to a Delivery Partner after applicable deductions.

Reconciliation means the process of verifying transactions before Settlement or Payout.

3. PAYMENT METHODS

Zopiq supports electronic payment methods including UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking, and other options made available on the Platform. Cash on Delivery is available only where offered by Zopiq. Payment processing is handled by authorised third-party payment service providers; Zopiq does not store complete card or UPI PIN credentials and is not responsible for failures, delays, or errors caused by external payment systems.

4. CUSTOMER PAYMENT STRUCTURE

4.1 40% Advance Payment

For every COD order, the Customer pays 40% of the total order value online as an Advance Payment before the order is confirmed. An order requiring an Advance Payment is not confirmed until the required amount has been successfully received.

4.2 60% on Delivery

The remaining 60% is paid in cash, or by another accepted method, upon successful delivery, if permitted by the Platform. Where a balance is due at delivery, the Customer must pay it before taking possession of the order, unless another arrangement is expressly permitted.

4.3 Non-Refundable Advance

Where a COD order fails due to Customer fault — refusal, unreachability, a wrong address, or abandonment after preparation — the 40% Advance Payment is non-refundable, as set out in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

4.4 Prepaid Orders

For prepaid orders, the full amount is collected online at the time of placement, subject to the same confirmation and refund principles.

5. DELIVERY FEES

Delivery fees are dynamic and may vary based on distance, demand, weather (including rain), time of day (including night), availability of Delivery Partners, government restrictions, and special events. Zopiq does not guarantee fixed delivery fees, and Customers are informed of the applicable charges before confirming an order.

6. RESTAURANT COMMISSION

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Zopiq charges a platform Commission of ₹10 per item sold through the Platform. Commission is deducted before Settlement. Zopiq may revise commission structures prospectively with reasonable notice to Restaurant Partners.

7. RESTAURANT SETTLEMENT

Settlements to Restaurant Partners are processed daily, subject to Reconciliation, applicable deductions, refunds, chargebacks, and legal requirements.

7.1 Deductions

Settlements are net of Commission, refunds or adjustments attributable to the Restaurant Partner, and any applicable taxes or statutory deductions.

7.2 Settlement Reports

Settlement reports are made available through the Restaurant Partner Application or dashboard, so Restaurant Partners can review amounts and deductions.

7.3 Withholding

Zopiq may withhold or adjust Settlements where fraud, duplicate transactions, or policy violations are reasonably suspected, pending Investigation and subject to applicable law.

8. DELIVERY PARTNER PAYOUTS

Payouts to Delivery Partners are processed daily, subject to verification of completed deliveries and applicable deductions or adjustments.

8.1 Composition

Payouts comprise delivery fees for completed deliveries and any applicable incentives, net of applicable deductions and taxes.

8.2 Records

Earnings and settlement history are available in the Delivery Partner Application.

8.3 Withholding

Zopiq may withhold or adjust Payouts where fraud, duplicate deliveries, or policy violations are reasonably suspected, pending Investigation and subject to applicable law.

9. FAILED PAYMENTS

Where a payment fails or an amount is debited without a confirmed order, the amount is generally reversed by the payment provider within its standard timelines. If a reversal does not occur automatically, the Customer should contact Customer Support with the transaction reference for reconciliation. In the event of a payment processing issue affecting Settlement, Zopiq may deduct a platform processing fee of ₹10, and the remaining eligible amount is settled to the Restaurant Partner in accordance with the settlement process, subject to applicable laws and Reconciliation.

10. REFUND SETTLEMENT

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method unless another method is expressly agreed, in accordance with the Refund & Cancellation Policy. Refunds or adjustments attributable to a Restaurant Partner may be deducted from that Restaurant Partner's Settlements. The time for a refund to reflect depends on the payment provider and the Customer's bank.

11. TAXES

Amounts may be subject to applicable taxes, including Goods and Services Tax, as required by law. Restaurant Partners and Delivery Partners are responsible for their own tax obligations arising from their activities on the Platform. Zopiq applies tax treatment and deductions as required by applicable law and makes relevant records available where appropriate.

12. RECONCILIATION

Before Settlements and Payouts are finalised, Zopiq reconciles transactions to verify completed orders and deliveries, apply deductions, and account for refunds and chargebacks. Reconciliation protects all parties against errors and fraud, and may cause short, reasonable delays where verification is required.

13. PAYMENT AND SETTLEMENT DISPUTES

Disputes relating to payments, Settlements, or Payouts should be raised through in-app support or the contact details in this Policy, with relevant order and settlement references.

13.1 Handling

Zopiq investigates payment and settlement disputes, may request supporting information, and communicates an outcome. Users are expected to cooperate and provide reasonably requested information.

13.2 Chargebacks

Customers are encouraged to contact support before initiating a chargeback. Chargeback fraud — reversing payment for a validly delivered order — is prohibited and may result in withheld settlements, suspension, or legal action.

15. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Zopiq may update this Policy prospectively to reflect changes in law, payment methods, or commercial terms, with reasonable notice where appropriate. Continued use of the Platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
