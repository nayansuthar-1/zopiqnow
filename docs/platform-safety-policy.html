1. INTRODUCTION

This Platform Safety Policy ("Policy") sets out how Zopiq, operated by Hybrid Monks LLP ("Zopiq", "Company", "we", "our", or "us"), promotes the safety of Customers, Restaurant Partners, and Delivery Partners, and how the Platform responds to safety risks, threats, and harmful conduct. It supplements the Community Guidelines, Terms & Conditions, and Partner Agreements.

Safety is a shared responsibility. Zopiq provides tools and standards, and expects all Users to act responsibly and lawfully.

2. DEFINITIONS

Safety Incident means any event involving actual or threatened harm to a person, or a serious risk to safety, in connection with the Platform.

Emergency means a situation requiring immediate action to protect life, health, or safety.

Harmful Conduct means threats, harassment, violence, or other behaviour that endangers a person.

3. GENERAL SAFETY PRINCIPLES

Our safety approach is guided by the following principles:

  • Personal safety takes priority over completing any order or delivery.
  • All Users are entitled to be treated without threats, violence, or harassment.
  • Serious safety risks are escalated and, where appropriate, reported to authorities.
  • Safety tools and reporting channels are provided within the applications.

4. CUSTOMER SAFETY

Zopiq supports Customer safety through measures such as order tracking, in-app or masked communication where available, and reporting channels. Customers should provide accurate delivery details, choose safe handover points, protect their account credentials, and report any unsafe interaction with a Delivery Partner or restaurant through in-app support.

5. RESTAURANT SAFETY

Restaurant Partners are responsible for maintaining a safe environment for their staff and for Delivery Partners collecting orders. This includes safe pickup areas, respectful treatment of Delivery Partners, and compliance with health and safety and food safety laws. Restaurant Partners should report threats or unsafe conduct by any User to Zopiq.

6. DELIVERY PARTNER SAFETY

Delivery Partner safety is a priority given the nature of the work.

6.1 Safe Practices

  • Obey traffic laws, wear a helmet and required safety gear, and avoid fatigue.
  • Never work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Be cautious at unfamiliar or poorly lit locations, especially at night.
  • Use in-app safety features and reporting channels where available.

6.2 Right to Decline Unsafe Situations

Delivery Partners should not put themselves at risk to complete a delivery. Where a location or situation is unsafe, they should follow the app's process for unsafe situations and contact support.

7. FOOD SAFETY

Food safety is primarily the responsibility of Restaurant Partners, who must comply with applicable food safety laws, maintain hygiene, use fresh ingredients, and package food securely. Delivery Partners must handle sealed orders carefully and never tamper with or consume food. Zopiq does not prepare food and does not inspect or guarantee food quality, but takes food-safety complaints seriously and investigates reports of unsafe, contaminated, or spoiled food.

8. THREATS, HARASSMENT, AND VIOLENCE

Zopiq has zero tolerance for threats, harassment, and violence.

8.1 Prohibited Conduct

Prohibited conduct includes threats of harm, intimidation, stalking, sexual harassment, hate speech, physical violence, and any conduct that endangers another person. This applies to all interactions connected with the Platform.

8.2 Response

Reports of threats, harassment, or violence are treated with priority. Zopiq may suspend or terminate accounts immediately where necessary to protect safety, preserve relevant information, and report serious matters to law enforcement.

9. ILLEGAL PRODUCTS AND ACTIVITIES

The Platform must not be used to order, list, sell, or distribute prohibited or illegal items, including alcoholic beverages (where not permitted), tobacco and nicotine products, illegal drugs or controlled substances, weapons, explosives, hazardous chemicals, counterfeit goods, and stolen property. Zopiq may remove listings, suspend or terminate accounts, and report unlawful activity to authorities.

10. EMERGENCY RESPONSE

In an Emergency, personal safety comes first.

10.1 Immediate Steps

Any person facing an Emergency should contact local emergency services where appropriate, move to safety, and use any in-app safety or SOS features that are available. Users should inform Zopiq support as soon as it is safe to do so.

10.2 Zopiq's Role

On becoming aware of a Safety Incident, Zopiq will take reasonable steps to support affected Users, manage the affected order or delivery, preserve relevant information, and cooperate with authorities where required by law. Zopiq is an intermediary and cannot guarantee prevention of all incidents, but is committed to responding responsibly.

11. REPORTING SAFETY CONCERNS

Users can report safety concerns through in-app support, email to hello@hybridmonks.com, or phone at +91 96608 77411. Reports should include relevant details such as the order reference, location, and a description of the concern. Safety reports are prioritised and handled sensitively, consistent with the Privacy Policy.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Zopiq may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, operations, or safety practices. Material changes will be communicated through the Platform or by other reasonable means. The "Last Updated" date on the cover page indicates the latest version.
