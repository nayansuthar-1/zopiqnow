1. INTRODUCTION

This Restaurant Partner Handbook ("Handbook") is a practical operational guide for restaurants, cloud kitchens, cafés, and food vendors partnering with Zopiq, operated by Hybrid Monks LLP ("Zopiq", "Company", "we", "our", or "us"). It explains how to onboard, manage listings, prepare and package food, handle pickups and complaints, and maintain the quality standards expected on the Platform.

This Handbook is operational guidance and supplements the Restaurant Partner Agreement, Terms & Conditions, Merchant SLA, and other policies. Where this Handbook and a binding agreement differ, the agreement prevails.

2. DEFINITIONS

Restaurant Partner Application means the app or dashboard through which you manage listings, orders, and settlements.

Preparation Time means the time from order acceptance to the order being ready for pickup.

Pickup means collection of a prepared order by a Delivery Partner.

SLA means the service levels set out in the Merchant Service Level Agreement.

3. RESTAURANT ONBOARDING

Onboarding brings your restaurant onto the Platform in a compliant, ready-to-operate state.

3.1 Documents Required

You will typically be asked to provide the following, subject to the Restaurant Verification Policy:

  • FSSAI registration or licence (as applicable to your business).
  • GST registration details (where applicable).
  • PAN (where required).
  • Bank account details for settlements.
  • Business registration or ownership details.
  • Menu, pricing, and operating hours.

3.2 Onboarding Steps

The typical onboarding sequence is:

  • Register and submit business and legal information.
  • Complete verification of licences and bank details.
  • Build your menu with accurate items, descriptions, prices, and images.
  • Configure operating hours and preparation times.
  • Review and accept the Restaurant Partner Agreement and applicable policies.
  • Go live once verification and setup are complete.

3.3 Keeping Details Current

You are responsible for keeping licences, bank details, menus, and operating hours accurate and up to date. Outdated information is a common cause of failed orders and settlement delays.

4. MENU STANDARDS

A clear, accurate menu drives orders and reduces disputes.

4.1 Item Accuracy

Each menu item should have an accurate name, a clear description, the correct price, and correct availability. Portion sizes and key ingredients should be described where relevant.

4.2 Pricing

Prices displayed on the Platform must match what you intend to charge. Do not list misleading prices or use the Platform to advertise items you cannot fulfil.

4.3 Categories and Structure

Organise items into logical categories (for example, starters, mains, beverages, desserts) so Customers can navigate easily. Keep bestsellers visible and remove discontinued items promptly.

4.4 Allergen and Dietary Information

Where reasonably practicable, indicate allergen and dietary information (for example, vegetarian, contains nuts). You remain solely responsible for the accuracy of allergen and ingredient information.

5. FOOD PHOTOGRAPHY GUIDELINES

Good images increase orders and set accurate expectations.

5.1 Quality Basics

  • Use natural, even lighting where possible; avoid harsh shadows.
  • Photograph the actual dish as served, not a stock or unrepresentative image.
  • Keep the background clean and uncluttered.
  • Use a consistent style across your menu for a professional look.

5.2 Accuracy

Images must reflect the real dish, portion, and presentation. Misleading images are a common source of complaints and may be removed.

5.3 Rights

Only upload images you own or are licensed to use. By uploading, you confirm you have the necessary rights, consistent with the Intellectual Property Policy.

6. PREPARATION STANDARDS

6.1 Timeliness

Once you accept an order, prepare it within the estimated preparation time. Consistent, realistic preparation times improve Customer experience and delivery coordination.

6.2 Consistency

Prepare each dish to a consistent recipe, portion, and presentation so that Customers receive what they expect every time.

6.3 Order Accuracy

Check each order against the ticket before handover: correct items, quantities, customisations, and any included utensils or condiments. Order accuracy is one of the strongest drivers of Customer satisfaction.

7. PACKAGING STANDARDS

Secure packaging protects food quality during transit and reduces spillage and damage complaints.

7.1 Leak and Spill Prevention

  • Use containers appropriate to the dish; seal liquids and gravies securely.
  • Separate wet and dry items where possible.
  • Avoid overfilling containers.

7.2 Temperature and Freshness

Package hot and cold items appropriately to maintain temperature and freshness. Keep items that should remain separate (for example, ice cream and hot dishes) in suitable packaging.

7.3 Tamper-Evidence and Labelling

Where possible, use tamper-evident sealing and label bags with the order reference. This supports food safety and helps Delivery Partners hand over the correct order.

8. KITCHEN HYGIENE

Hygiene is a legal obligation and a core expectation on the Platform.

8.1 General Practices

  • Maintain clean preparation surfaces, equipment, and utensils.
  • Ensure staff follow handwashing and personal hygiene practices.
  • Store ingredients at correct temperatures and observe use-by dates.
  • Prevent cross-contamination between raw and cooked foods and known allergens.

8.2 Compliance

You must comply with applicable food safety laws, including the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, and maintain a valid FSSAI licence where required. Zopiq does not inspect or guarantee food quality; responsibility rests with the Restaurant Partner.

9. PICKUP WORKFLOW

A smooth pickup keeps deliveries on time and food fresh.

9.1 Preparing for Pickup

Have the order packed, sealed, and labelled by the time the Delivery Partner arrives. Keep prepared orders in a designated handover area.

9.2 Handover

Confirm the order reference with the Delivery Partner and hand over the correct, complete order. Do not hand over incomplete orders; if an item is delayed, communicate promptly.

9.3 Managing Delays

If preparation is running late, update the order status where possible so the Delivery Partner and Customer are informed. Repeated avoidable delays affect your performance metrics.

10. COMPLAINT HANDLING

Handling complaints well protects your ratings and Customer relationships.

10.1 Common Complaint Types

Complaint Typical Cause Preventive Action
Missing item Order not checked before handover. Verify every order against the ticket at handover.
Wrong item Similar items confused; mislabelling. Label clearly; double-check customisations.
Spilled / leaked food Inadequate sealing or packaging. Seal liquids; avoid overfilling; use suitable containers.
Quality / freshness Delays, temperature loss, inconsistent prep. Prepare to time; package for temperature; keep recipes consistent.

10.2 Responding

Cooperate with Zopiq during complaint investigations, provide requested information promptly, and take corrective action to prevent recurrence.

11. REFUND HANDLING

Refunds are governed by the Refund & Cancellation Policy. Key points for Restaurant Partners:

  • Wrong item: a replacement or exchange is arranged where reasonably possible.
  • Missing item: a refund equal to the verified missing item value may apply.
  • Damaged or spilled food: a partial refund may apply after verification.
  • Refunds attributable to the restaurant may be deducted from your settlements.

Accurate preparation and packaging are the most effective way to minimise refund-related deductions.

12. RESTAURANT DASHBOARD USAGE

The Restaurant Partner Application is your operational control centre.

12.1 Core Functions

  • Receive, accept, and manage incoming orders.
  • Update item availability and toggle items on or off.
  • Manage operating hours and preparation times.
  • View settlement reports and payout information.
  • Access performance metrics and ratings.

12.2 Good Habits

Keep availability accurate in real time, respond to orders promptly, and review your settlement and performance reports regularly to catch issues early.

13. QUALITY EXPECTATIONS

Zopiq expects Restaurant Partners to consistently meet standards across the following dimensions:

Dimension Expectation
Order accuracy Correct items, quantities, and customisations every time.
Preparation time Orders ready within the stated preparation time.
Packaging Secure, leak-resistant, temperature-appropriate packaging.
Hygiene & safety Full compliance with food safety laws and hygienic practice.
Availability accuracy Menu and availability kept current to avoid failed orders.
Cancellations Kept to a minimum and only for genuine operational reasons.

14. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT

Where performance falls below expectations, Zopiq may provide guidance and improvement steps before applying restrictions.

14.1 Using Your Metrics

Review your acceptance rate, preparation times, cancellation rate, and ratings regularly. Small, consistent improvements — accurate availability, faster prep, better packaging — compound into stronger performance and visibility.

14.2 Improvement Support

Zopiq may share operational insights such as order volumes, peak times, average preparation times, and delivery performance to help you improve. Persistent or serious issues may lead to reduced visibility, temporary suspension, or removal under the Merchant SLA and Restaurant Partner Agreement.

15. BEST PRACTICES

A concise checklist for consistently strong performance:

  • Keep the menu, prices, and availability accurate at all times.
  • Use honest, high-quality images that reflect the real dish.
  • Accept orders promptly and prepare within your stated times.
  • Check every order for accuracy before handover.
  • Seal and label packaging to prevent spills and mix-ups.
  • Maintain strict hygiene and food safety compliance.
  • Respond constructively to complaints and act on feedback.
  • Review settlement and performance reports regularly.
