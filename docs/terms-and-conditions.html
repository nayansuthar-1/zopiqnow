12. CASH ON DELIVERY (COD) POLICY

To reduce fraudulent and abandoned orders, Zopiq requires Customers choosing Cash on Delivery to pay an advance amount.

12.1 Advance Payment

For every COD order:

  • The Customer shall pay 40% of the total order value online as an advance.
  • The remaining 60% shall be paid in cash or by another accepted method upon successful delivery, if permitted by the Platform.

12.2 Non-Refundable Advance

If a Customer:

  • refuses to accept the order,
  • is unavailable at the delivery address after reasonable delivery attempts,
  • provides an incorrect delivery address,
  • intentionally abandons the order, or
  • otherwise causes the order to fail after preparation,

the 40% advance payment shall be forfeited and shall not be refunded.

12.3 Restaurant Cancellation

If a Restaurant Partner cancels an order before preparation due to reasons attributable to the restaurant (such as stock unavailability or inability to fulfil the order), the Customer shall receive a refund of the advance payment in accordance with the applicable refund process.

12.4 Platform Rights

Repeated refusal of COD orders or abuse of the COD facility may result in:

  • temporary suspension of COD privileges,
  • mandatory prepaid-only orders, or
  • suspension or termination of the Customer account.

APPENDIX A – COD POLICY SUMMARY

  • Cash on Delivery orders require a 40% advance payment.
  • The remaining 60% is payable upon delivery (or by another method accepted by the Platform).
  • If the Customer refuses delivery, is unavailable after reasonable attempts, provides an incorrect address, or otherwise causes the order to fail after preparation, the 40% advance is non-refundable.
  • Repeated abuse of the COD facility may result in restriction or removal of COD privileges and/or suspension of the Customer account.

APPENDIX B – RESTAURANT COMMISSION

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

  • Zopiq charges a platform commission of ₹10 per item sold through the Platform.
  • Commission is deducted before settlement.
  • Zopiq may revise commission structures prospectively with reasonable notice to Restaurant Partners.

APPENDIX C – PAYMENT SETTLEMENTS

Restaurant Partners

  • Settlements are processed daily, subject to successful reconciliation, applicable deductions, and legal requirements.
  • Settlement reports will be made available through the Restaurant Partner application or dashboard.

Delivery Partners

  • Delivery Partner earnings are processed daily, subject to verification of completed deliveries and applicable deductions or adjustments.
  • Zopiq reserves the right to withhold or adjust payouts where fraud, duplicate deliveries, or policy violations are reasonably suspected, pending investigation.

APPENDIX D – PROHIBITED ITEMS

The following categories are prohibited on the Platform unless expressly permitted by applicable law and by Zopiq:

  • Alcoholic beverages
  • Tobacco and nicotine products
  • Illegal drugs or controlled substances
  • Weapons
  • Explosives
  • Hazardous chemicals
  • Counterfeit goods
  • Stolen property
  • Any other items prohibited by law or by Zopiq's policies

Restaurants must not list or sell prohibited items through the Platform.
