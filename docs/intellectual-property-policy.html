1. INTRODUCTION

This Intellectual Property Policy ("Policy") explains the ownership and permitted use of intellectual property in connection with the Zopiq platform, operated by Hybrid Monks LLP ("Zopiq", "Company", "we", "our", or "us"), and the procedure for reporting alleged infringement. It supplements the intellectual property provisions of the Terms & Conditions and the Partner Agreements.

2. DEFINITIONS

Platform IP means intellectual property owned by or licensed to Hybrid Monks LLP, including the Zopiq name, logos, software, source code, APIs, designs, and related materials.

User Content means content submitted by Users, such as reviews, images, and menu information.

Rights Holder means a person who owns or controls intellectual property rights.

Infringement means the unauthorised use of intellectual property in a manner that violates a Rights Holder's rights.

3. OWNERSHIP OF PLATFORM IP

All rights, title, and interest in and to the Platform, including the Zopiq name, logo, mobile applications, website, software, source code, APIs, UI/UX designs, icons, graphics, animations, databases, business processes, documentation, and related materials, are owned by or licensed to Hybrid Monks LLP and are protected under applicable copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret, and other intellectual property laws.

Nothing in this Policy or in the use of the Platform transfers ownership of any Platform IP to any User.

10. INFRINGEMENT REPORTING AND NOTICE PROCEDURE

Zopiq respects intellectual property rights and provides a procedure for Rights Holders to report alleged Infringement on the Platform.

10.1 How to Submit a Notice

A Rights Holder (or their authorised agent) may submit a notice of alleged Infringement to hello@hybridmonks.com or the registered office address. To enable Zopiq to act, the notice should include:

  • Identification of the work or right claimed to be infringed.
  • Identification of the allegedly infringing material and its location on the Platform.
  • The Rights Holder's contact details.
  • A statement that the complainant believes in good faith that the use is not authorised.
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and that the complainant is the Rights Holder or authorised to act on their behalf.

10.2 Action on a Valid Notice

On receiving a valid notice, Zopiq may remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material, notify the User who provided it where appropriate, and take further action consistent with its policies and applicable law.

10.3 Counter-Notice

A User whose content is removed may submit a counter-notice explaining why the content is not infringing, including their contact details and a good-faith statement. Zopiq may restore content where appropriate after considering the counter-notice, unless doing so would be unlawful.

10.4 Repeat Infringers

Zopiq may suspend or terminate the accounts of Users who repeatedly infringe intellectual property rights.

11. MISUSE OF THIS PROCEDURE

Submitting false or bad-faith notices or counter-notices is prohibited and may result in Enforcement Action and liability under applicable law. Zopiq is not obliged to act on notices that are incomplete or that do not appear to be made in good faith.
