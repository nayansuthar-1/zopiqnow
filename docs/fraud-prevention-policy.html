1. INTRODUCTION

This Fraud Prevention Policy ("Policy") describes how Zopiq, operated by Hybrid Monks LLP ("Zopiq", "Company", "we", "our", or "us"), detects, prevents, and responds to fraud and abuse on the Platform. It applies to Customers, Restaurant Partners, and Delivery Partners, and supplements the Terms & Conditions, Partner Agreements, and Payment & Settlement Policy.

Fraud harms Customers, honest partners, and the Platform. Zopiq takes a firm, evidence-based approach to fraud while respecting due process and applicable law.

2. DEFINITIONS

Fraud means any dishonest act intended to obtain an unfair or unlawful gain, or to cause loss to another, in connection with the Platform.

Abuse means misuse of Platform features, offers, or processes, whether or not it constitutes Fraud.

Investigation means the process by which Zopiq reviews suspected Fraud or Abuse.

Enforcement Action means any measure taken in response to Fraud or Abuse, including holds, restrictions, suspension, termination, or referral to authorities.

3. PRINCIPLES

Zopiq's approach to fraud is guided by the following principles:

  • Prevention first: reduce opportunities for Fraud through verification and controls.
  • Proportionality: responses match the severity and evidence of the conduct.
  • Fairness: affected Users are handled with due process, subject to safety and legal limits.
  • Privacy: personal data used in fraud prevention is handled under the Privacy Policy.
  • Compliance: serious Fraud may be reported to relevant authorities.

4. FAKE ORDERS

Placing fake orders, orders without intent to complete the purchase, or orders using false information is prohibited. Indicators include repeated refusals, unreachable Customers, invalid addresses, and abnormal ordering patterns. Zopiq may restrict COD, require prepayment, or suspend accounts engaged in fake ordering.

5. COD ABUSE

Cash on Delivery is offered subject to a mandatory 40% advance to reduce abandoned and fraudulent orders. Abuse includes repeatedly refusing COD deliveries, providing wrong addresses, or being deliberately unreachable after ordering.

5.1 Consequences

Repeated COD abuse may result in forfeiture of the non-refundable 40% advance (where the failure is the Customer's fault), temporary suspension of COD privileges, mandatory prepaid-only ordering, or account suspension, consistent with the Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6. COUPON AND REFERRAL ABUSE

Promotional offers and referral programs are for genuine use by eligible Users. Prohibited conduct includes creating multiple or fake accounts to claim offers, exploiting technical errors, self-referral, and coordinated schemes to extract promotional value. Zopiq may reverse improperly obtained benefits and take Enforcement Action.

7. RESTAURANT FRAUD

Restaurant-side Fraud includes falsified licences, misleading listings intended to deceive, manipulation of orders or settlements, collusion to generate fake orders, and billing for items not provided. Zopiq may withhold settlements pending Investigation, remove listings, and suspend or terminate Restaurant Partners engaged in Fraud, and may report unlawful conduct.

8. DELIVERY FRAUD

Delivery-side Fraud includes marking deliveries as completed without delivering, delivery theft or tampering, collusion with Customers or restaurants, manipulation of location or proof-of-delivery, and generating duplicate or fictitious deliveries. Zopiq may withhold or adjust payouts pending Investigation and deactivate Delivery Partners engaged in Fraud.

9. PAYMENT FRAUD

Payment Fraud includes unauthorised use of payment instruments, chargeback fraud, and manipulation of refunds or settlements. Zopiq works with authorised payment providers, does not store complete card or UPI PIN credentials, and may hold or reverse transactions, restrict payment methods, and take Enforcement Action where Payment Fraud is reasonably suspected.

10. IDENTITY FRAUD

Identity Fraud includes providing false identity information, using another person's identity or documents, and submitting forged documents. Zopiq may require identity verification, suspend accounts pending verification, and terminate accounts and refer matters to authorities where Identity Fraud is established.

11. ACCOUNT VERIFICATION AND CONTROLS

To prevent Fraud, Zopiq may implement verification and detection measures, including:

  • Device and IP analysis.
  • Payment risk assessment.
  • Multiple-account detection.
  • Order and delivery pattern analysis.
  • Identity and location verification.
  • Behavioural analytics.

These measures are applied consistently with the Privacy Policy and principles of data minimization.

12. INVESTIGATION PROCEDURES

Where Fraud or Abuse is reasonably suspected, Zopiq may investigate.

12.1 Interim Measures

During an Investigation, Zopiq may hold or delay payments and settlements, restrict payment methods, disable COD, request additional verification, or temporarily suspend accounts, to the extent reasonably necessary and subject to applicable law.

12.2 Conducting the Investigation

Zopiq reviews relevant data and evidence, may request information from affected Users, and assesses the matter objectively. Users are expected to cooperate and provide reasonably requested information.

12.3 Outcomes

Finding Typical Outcome
No Fraud found Interim measures lifted; normal service resumes.
Minor Abuse Warning, reversal of improper benefit, or feature restriction.
Serious or repeated Fraud Suspension or permanent termination; settlement adjustments.
Unlawful conduct Referral to relevant authorities and possible legal action.

13. CONSEQUENCES AND ENFORCEMENT

Enforcement Action is proportionate to the conduct and may include warnings, reversal of improperly obtained value, restriction of features or payment methods, withholding or adjustment of settlements or payouts, suspension, permanent termination, and referral to law enforcement. Providing false information, forged documents, or engaging in payment fraud may lead to permanent account termination and legal action.

14. APPEALS

A User affected by Enforcement Action may appeal through in-app support or the contact details in this Policy, providing relevant information. Zopiq will review appeals fairly and within a reasonable timeframe. Decisions on serious Fraud may be final where necessary to protect users or comply with law.

15. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Zopiq may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, fraud patterns, or technology. Material changes will be communicated through the Platform or by other reasonable means. The "Last Updated" date on the cover page indicates the latest version.
